1. Page typesetting

The configuration is injected from appsettings.json by WebSharper.MathJax.ConfigResource before the component loads. MathJax typesets the math already on the page; typeset(), typesetPromise() and typesetClear() handle later changes.

Display math: $$x = {-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac} \over 2a}$$

Inline math: $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$ and \(\int_0^1 x^2 dx\).
